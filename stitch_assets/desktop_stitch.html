Frequently Asked Questions by Exhibitors.

Everything you need to know about our Cairo on-ground operational protocols before confirming your reservation.

Can you guarantee verified meetings with legitimate Egyptian importers? check_circle

Yes. When selecting our Package 02 (Business Show + Buyer Outreach), we provide a pre-show dossier containing verified company names, commercial registration details, and specific procurement volume interests. We do not count consumer walk-ins as meetings; only scheduled B2B sit-downs with qualified decision-makers.

What if our international team cannot travel to Cairo for Food Africa? check_circle

We provide full Remote Stand Execution. You can ship your promotional displays and product samples directly to our Cairo office. Our trained staff will set up your booth, welcome attendees, qualify commercial inquiries, and initiate live video conferences between pre-vetted buyers and your executives at your home headquarters.

How early should we book our Food Africa exhibition support team? check_circle

To ensure thorough pre-show buyer outreach, we strongly recommend initiating your support plan 6 to 8 weeks prior to the exhibition dates (by October 2026). Specialized interpreters versed in technical agricultural and FMCG terms are typically reserved completely by early November.

Can we hire a commercial interpreter for just 1 or 2 specific days? check_circle

While the majority of clients retain our interpreters for the full 4-day run of the show to maintain consistency with recurring buyer visits, partial bookings (minimum 2 consecutive days) are accommodated based on schedule availability.

Do you assist with Egyptian food regulatory (NFSA) inquiries? check_circle

Yes. Our permanent Cairo business development practice works routinely alongside registered Egyptian customs and clearing brokers. We provide clear guidance on National Food Safety Authority (NFSA) registration protocols, required Arabic labeling, and lab testing parameters for imported foodstuffs.
